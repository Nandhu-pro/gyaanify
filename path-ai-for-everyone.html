What generative AI is

Generative AI creates new text, images, audio or code based on patterns it learned from large amounts of data. Chat assistants predict useful responses to what you ask.

It's powerful for drafting, summarising, explaining and brainstorming. It can also be confidently wrong, so treat its answers as a strong first draft, not the final truth.

Key takeaways

  • AI generates content from learned patterns
  • Great for drafts and explanations
  • Always check important facts
Lesson 2 of 42 min read

Writing good prompts

Clear prompts get better answers. Say who the answer is for, what you want, and the format you need. For example: "Explain cloud computing to a first-year student in five bullet points with one everyday example."

Give context and examples, ask for step-by-step reasoning on hard problems, and refine with follow-up questions instead of starting over.

Key takeaways

  • State the audience, task and format
  • Give context and examples
  • Improve answers with follow-ups
Lesson 3 of 42 min read

Using AI safely

Don't paste passwords, OTPs, ID numbers, bank details, private company code or client data into public AI tools. If you wouldn't post it publicly, don't paste it.

Download AI apps only from official stores, since fake look-alike apps exist. At work, follow your organisation's AI policy, and don't trust AI-made screenshots or documents as proof.

Key takeaways

  • Never share secrets or personal data
  • Use official apps only
  • Follow your organisation's AI policy
Lesson 4 of 42 min read

AI agents and prompt injection

AI agents can take actions, such as reading email, browsing or running tools. Prompt injection is when hidden instructions inside a web page, email or document try to trick the agent into leaking data or doing something you didn't ask.

Safe agent design gives agents only the access they need, asks a human to approve risky actions and keeps logs. You can try prompt injection yourself in the free Gandalf game by Lakera.

Key takeaways

  • Agents act, so mistakes have impact
  • Hidden instructions can hijack them
  • Least privilege and human approval keep them safe
